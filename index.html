TURTLES & REEF

Two unforgettable natural experiences close to Bargara.

ABOUT 10 MINUTES FROM BARGARA
01

Mon Repos Turtle Centre

Visit one of the world’s most important loggerhead turtle nesting areas. Learn about marine turtle conservation year-round, and in season join a ranger-guided night encounter for the chance to see nesting turtles or hatchlings making their way to the sea.

Turtle Encounter season: generally November to late March. Bookings and seasonal access conditions apply.

Explore Mon Repos →
SOUTHERN GREAT BARRIER REEF
02

Great Barrier Reef adventures

Use Bargara as your coastal base for the Southern Great Barrier Reef. Discover coral gardens, turtles and marine life around Lady Elliot Island and Lady Musgrave Island, with day-trip and overnight experiences available from the Bundaberg region.

Ideas: snorkelling, diving, glass-bottom boat trips, island walks and reef wildlife encounters.

Discover the Southern Great Barrier Reef →
EXPLORE BUNDABERG

A little further from the beach, there is plenty more to discover.

ICONIC BUNDABERG EXPERIENCE
01

Bundaberg Rum Distillery

Visit the home of Bundaberg Rum and discover the history and craft behind one of Australia’s most recognisable spirits. The visitor experience includes museum displays, distillery tours and the chance to explore the famous Bondstore.

Good to know: tour places can be limited, so booking ahead is a good idea.

Visit Bundaberg Rum →
BUNDABERG DAY OUT
02

Botanic Gardens & Hinkler Hall

Combine the landscaped Bundaberg Botanic Gardens with Hinkler Hall of Aviation, an interactive attraction celebrating pioneering Australian aviator Bert Hinkler. It makes an easy addition to a day exploring Bundaberg.

Pair it with: lunch in town, the rum distillery or a local produce stop on the way back to Bargara.
