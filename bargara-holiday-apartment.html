Planning your stay

A self-contained Bargara holiday, with space to settle in.

Apartment 101 is a ground-floor, two-bedroom holiday apartment suited to up to five guests. The renovated kitchen makes it easy to prepare meals during your stay, while the living areas and private patio give everyone room to relax after a day exploring Bargara.

Beach days and local cafés

Make the most of Bargara's beaches, seaside walks and nearby places to eat. Return to the apartment for a relaxed evening rather than having to organise every meal out.

Time around the Bundaberg coast

Use Bargara as a base for exploring the local coastline. If you're interested in the seasonal Mon Repos turtle experience, check official tour dates and availability before you travel.
